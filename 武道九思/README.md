## 作品简介 / About

架空中古武侠。山村少年陈九斤（大号九思）从老槐树下看牛使力悟出"劲走腰马、力从地起"的武道真意，身负父亲留下的残页拳谱，走镖路、过青山道、入江湖庙堂，最后明白——武道的尽头是回家。

A wuxia tale set in an imagined ancient era. A village boy, Chen Jiujin (formal name Jiusi), learns the true meaning of martial arts by watching an ox use its strength under an old locust tree. Carrying his father's half-torn boxing manual, he walks the escort roads, crosses Qingshan Road, and enters the world of jianghu and court — until he understands that the end of the martial way is going home.
